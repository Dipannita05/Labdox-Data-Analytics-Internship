# 📱 Svarajya App — Data & Module Audit

## 📌 Project Overview

As part of my Data Analyst Internship at **Labdox Pvt. Ltd.**, I worked on the **Svarajya App Data & Module Audit**, focusing on documenting application modules, verifying functionality, recording issues, and maintaining evidence-based audit documentation.

The work involved reviewing the live application, maintaining structured audit information, performing functional verification, and capturing screenshot-based evidence for observed bugs and findings.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Document and maintain an inventory of application modules.
* Review and verify functionality across relevant application modules.
* Identify and document bugs and unexpected behaviour.
* Capture evidence screenshots for observed issues.
* Maintain structured audit documentation.
* Support application review through evidence-based findings.

---

## 🔎 Work Performed

### 📋 Module Inventory

Created and maintained a structured **Module Inventory** covering the application's modules and relevant information.

The repository includes **14 screenshots** documenting the module inventory work.

### 🐞 Bug Verification & Evidence

Performed functional checks on the live Svarajya application and documented observed bugs, issues, and verification findings through screenshots.

The repository includes **23 evidence screenshots**, covering different bugs and observations identified during the audit.

The original filenames have been retained to maintain traceability with the audit work.

### 🗂️ Audit Worksheet

Worked with structured audit worksheet documentation for recording application-related information and findings.

**3 screenshots** of the relevant worksheet sections are included as supporting evidence.
