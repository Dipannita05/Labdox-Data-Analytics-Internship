# 🎁 Amit Ke Circuits — Business Growth Toolkit

A business growth toolkit developed during my **Data Analyst Internship at Labdox** for **Amit Ke Circuits**, focusing on how thoughtful gifting can help Indian SMEs build stronger professional relationships, improve client recall, encourage referrals, and strengthen long-term business relationships.

## 🚀 Project Overview

The project explores **relationship building through thoughtful gifting** as a year-round business strategy rather than limiting gifting to major festivals.

The work combines research, practical recommendations, a gifting calendar, budget-based gift selection, professional gifting guidelines, and an actionable Excel-based toolkit.

**Research → Insights → Business Recommendations → Practical Toolkit**

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand how gifting can support professional relationship building
* Identify key business relationships where gifting can add value
* Explore gifting opportunities beyond traditional festivals
* Develop practical gift recommendations across different budgets
* Provide guidelines for thoughtful and culturally sensitive gifting
* Create an actionable toolkit for planning and tracking business gifting

## 🔎 Research & Analysis

The research focused on:

* Corporate gifting trends in India
* Client and referral partner relationships
* Employee and vendor relationships
* Year-round gifting occasions
* Gift selection and budget considerations
* Personalisation and experience-based gifting
* Cultural sensitivity in professional gifting
* Ethical gifting practices
* Common mistakes in business gifting

The research was used to develop practical recommendations specifically relevant to **Indian SME owners, consultants, traders, service providers, and entrepreneurs**.

## 🧰 Business Toolkit

The project includes a practical Excel-based toolkit containing:

* **Gifting Action Checklist**
* **Gifting Tracker**
* **Gift Selection Matrix**
* **Gifting Occasion Calendar**

The toolkit is designed to help business owners plan gifting activities, track important occasions, select suitable gifts, and maintain consistent relationship-building efforts throughout the year.

## 📄 Final Deliverable

The final Business Growth Toolkit presents:

* Why thoughtful gifting matters
* Types of business relationships where gifting helps
* Year-round gifting occasions
* Budget-based gift recommendations
* Professional gifting dos and don'ts
* Sample messages for different occasions
* Common gifting mistakes
* Quick implementation plan
* Practical tips and key takeaways

## 💡 Key Takeaways

* Thoughtful gifting can support stronger professional relationships when timing and personalisation are considered.
* Relationship-building opportunities extend beyond major festive occasions.
* Small, personalised gestures can be used throughout the year.
* A structured gifting calendar can help businesses consistently identify and act on important relationship moments.
* Cultural sensitivity and ethical considerations are important when selecting professional gifts.
