# 🌱 Dharati Dhan — Competitor Intelligence & Digital Presence Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A competitor intelligence and digital presence analysis project completed during my **Data Analyst Internship at Labdox** for Dharati Dhan, an agricultural retail business in Ranchi.

As the **Digital Presence, E-Commerce & Dashboard Lead**, I analyzed competitors across digital platforms, evaluated e-commerce readiness, performed digital maturity analysis, and developed a Power BI dashboard to present the findings.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify and analyze relevant competitors in the Ranchi market
* Evaluate competitor websites and digital platforms
* Assess social media and WhatsApp Business presence
* Analyze e-commerce and digital sales readiness
* Identify digital gaps and competitive opportunities
* Present research findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

The research covered:

* Competitor identification and verification
* Product and market positioning
* Website and e-commerce analysis
* Social media presence
* WhatsApp Business adoption
* IndiaMART, Justdial and TradeIndia presence
* Amazon and Flipkart presence
* Digital maturity scoring
* Competitive gap analysis
* Opportunity identification

The analysis used a structured competitor research matrix and a **10-channel digital presence framework** to compare competitors consistently.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Competitor Identification → Research & Verification → Digital Presence Analysis → E-Commerce Assessment → Digital Maturity Scoring → Gap Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive **two-page Power BI dashboard** to transform the competitor research into visual business insights.

The dashboard covers:

* Competitor KPIs
* Digital platform presence
* E-commerce readiness
* Competitor comparison
* Digital maturity analysis
* Detailed competitor matrix

The dashboard was developed using the structured competitor research collected and analyzed in Excel.

## 💡 Key Insights

* Digital maturity varied considerably across competitors.
* Social media and WhatsApp were more commonly adopted than complete e-commerce infrastructure.
* Website presence did not always indicate actual online selling capability.
* Marketplace presence across Amazon and Flipkart was relatively limited.
* Multi-platform digital visibility created a competitive advantage.
* Several digital gaps and opportunities were identified for Dharati Dhan.
