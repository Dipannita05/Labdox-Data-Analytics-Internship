# 💼 Labdox — Data Analyst Internship

## 📌 Internship Overview

This repository showcases the work completed during my **Data Analyst Internship at Labdox Pvt. Ltd.**, undertaken from **June to July 2026**.

The internship provided practical exposure to **data analysis, competitor intelligence, Power BI reporting, application auditing, business research, structured documentation, and evidence-based analysis**.

The repository contains three projects covering different aspects of analytical and business-oriented work.

---

## 🎯 Internship Objectives

* Apply data analytics concepts to real-world business tasks.
* Develop practical experience with **Excel and Power BI**.
* Perform competitor and digital presence analysis.
* Conduct structured application auditing and functional verification.
* Research business growth opportunities and translate findings into practical recommendations.
* Build professional documentation and evidence-based deliverables.

---

## 📂 Projects

### 🌱 1. Dharati Dhan — Competitor Intelligence & Digital Presence Analysis

A competitor intelligence project focused on evaluating the **digital and platform presence** of Dharati Dhan and its competitors.

**Key Work:**

* Competitor identification and verification
* Product and market positioning analysis
* Website and e-commerce presence analysis
* Social media and messaging platform presence
* Marketplace and business-directory analysis
* Digital maturity scoring
* Competitive gap identification
* Power BI dashboard development

**Tools:** Excel, Power BI, Google Sheets, Google Maps, web research

👉 See the **Dharati Dhan Competitor Intelligence** folder for the complete project documentation and dashboard.

---

### 📱 2. Svarajya App — Data & Module Audit

An application audit project involving structured review and documentation of the **Svarajya web application**.

**Key Work:**

* Module inventory documentation
* Application and screen review
* Functional verification
* Bug identification and verification
* Evidence screenshot collection
* Structured audit worksheet documentation
* Evidence-based issue reporting

**Tools:** Google Sheets, Excel, Google Chrome, Svarajya Web Application

👉 See the **Svarajya App Data & Module Audit** folder for the audit evidence and documentation.

---

### 🎁 3. Amit Ke Circuits — Business Growth Toolkit

A business research and toolkit development project focused on **relationship building through thoughtful gifting** for Indian SMEs and business professionals.

**Key Work:**

* Business research
* Relationship-based gifting analysis
* Gifting occasion planning
* Budget-based gift selection
* Practical gifting guidelines
* Sample communication messages
* Implementation planning
* Excel-based gifting toolkit development

**Tools:** Microsoft Word, Excel, Google research and documentation tools

👉 See the **Amit Ke Circuits Business Growth Toolkit** folder for the research, final deliverable, and toolkit.
